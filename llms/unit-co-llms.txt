# Unit

> **Unit is a financial infrastructure platform for technology companies that want to build products that let their customers store, move, spend, and borrow money inside their product.**

Unit provides accounts and wallets, money movement, card issuing, capital, operational tooling, and program management capabilities through a unified platform.

Companies can build directly on the Unit Platform using APIs and components, with control over their product experience and operating model. For companies that prioritize speed to market and lower operational ownership, Unit also offers Managed Solutions for Banking and Bill Pay. Both approaches run on the same core infrastructure, allowing companies to deepen their integration over time without moving to a different platform.

Unit is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services are provided by Unit's bank partners.

## The Unit Platform

[The Unit Platform](https://www.unit.co/platform-overview) is enterprise-grade financial infrastructure for building and operating financial products. It combines financial building blocks, operational tooling, program management capabilities, and direct connectivity to financial networks and bank partners.

### Financial building blocks

Unit's four core financial building blocks are Accounts & Wallets, Money Movement, Card Issuing, and Capital. They run on a unified infrastructure and native ledger, so companies can combine capabilities without stitching together separate systems.

* [Accounts & Wallets](https://www.unit.co/accounts-and-wallets): Create deposit accounts and wallet accounts held in FBO structures. Support use cases including operating accounts, customer wallets, sub-accounts, and multi-party fund flows, with real-time balances and transaction data.
* [Money Movement](https://www.unit.co/money-movement): Send and receive money using payment capabilities including ACH, wires, real-time payments, checks, and international payments, with settlement-level transaction visibility.
* [Card Issuing](https://www.unit.co/card-issuing): Issue physical and virtual cards with configurable controls, real-time authorization capabilities, transaction data, and card lifecycle management.
* [Capital](https://www.unit.co/lending): Build business capital and credit products including charge cards, revolving credit cards, lines of credit, merchant cash advances, invoice factoring, and instant access to funds.

### Architecture

Unit's infrastructure is designed to give companies direct access to the systems beneath their financial products.

* **Native ledger:** Accounts, transactions, and balances are maintained on Unit's native ledger, with state changes tied to transaction and network events.
* **Direct payment network connectivity:** Unit is a registered Federal Reserve service provider with direct connectivity to Federal Reserve systems on behalf of partner banks.
* **Direct card network connectivity:** Unit connects directly to card networks for card issuing and transaction processing.
* **Multi-bank infrastructure:** Companies can work with one or multiple Unit bank partners through the same infrastructure.
* **Operational tooling:** The Unit Dashboard and APIs provide teams with access to customer, account, payment, card, transaction, compliance, and operational data.
* **Configurable program management:** Responsibilities for compliance, risk, operations, reporting, support, and other program functions depend on how a company builds on Unit and its agreements with Unit and its bank partners.

For a technical overview, see the [Enterprise-Grade Financial Infrastructure Guide](https://www.unit.co/guides/enterprise-grade-financial-infrastructure).

For guidance on choosing an operating model, see [Configuring Your Build on Unit](https://www.unit.co/guides/configuring-your-build).

## Managed Solutions

[Managed Solutions](https://www.unit.co/managed-solutions) are financial products built on Unit's infrastructure and operationalized by Unit for companies that prioritize speed to market and lower technical and operational ownership.

Managed Solutions currently include Banking and Bill Pay.

* [Banking](https://www.unit.co/managed-solutions-banking): The Banking module within Unit's Managed Solutions. Give customers accounts, cards, money movement, and tools to organize and manage their funds directly inside a software product.
* [Bill Pay](https://www.unit.co/managed-solutions-bill-pay): The Bill Pay module within Unit's Managed Solutions. Give customers tools for bill capture, vendor management, approvals, payment scheduling, payments, and accounts payable workflows.

Managed Solutions and direct Platform implementations use the same underlying Unit infrastructure. Companies can expand their use of Unit and take on additional technical or operational ownership as their requirements evolve.

## Program Management

Building and operating financial products involves more than APIs. Program management includes the compliance, risk, operations, reporting, customer support, and other workflows required to keep a financial product running.

Unit provides infrastructure and tooling for capabilities including:

* Identity verification and KYC/KYB
* AML monitoring and case management
* Fraud and payment decisioning
* Dispute handling
* Reconciliation
* Statements and tax forms
* Bank reporting
* Customer and transaction operations
* Sensitive financial data management

Which responsibilities Unit, the company, and its bank partners own depends on the implementation and applicable agreements.

See [Beyond the API: What to Look for When Building Financial Products](https://www.unit.co/guides/embedded-finance-program-management) for more detail.

* **Capital is a core Platform capability. It is not a Managed Solution.**
* Unit's **Managed Solutions** currently consist of **Banking and Bill Pay**.
* Building directly on the **Unit Platform** provides greater configurability and control over the product and operating model.
* Managed Solutions and direct Platform implementations run on the **same core infrastructure**.
* Program management responsibilities depend on the implementation and applicable agreements.
* Unit is **not a bank**. Banking services are provided by Unit's bank partners.
